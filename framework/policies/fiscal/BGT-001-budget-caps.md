---
id: BGT-001
title: Enforce Firm Monthly Budget Caps
domain: fiscal
status: proposed
scope: budget
rules:
  - hard_cap: "Monthly spend may not exceed the allocated cap; system will auto‑throttle new runs at 95% utilization."
  - override_process: "Any cap override requires explicit clerk approval and must be logged with justification."
  - reporting: "Daily spend reports are sent to the Intake team; any breach triggers an automatic alert."
---
The policy establishes a non‑negotiable monthly budget ceiling and defines an automatic throttling mechanism that activates once spend reaches 95% of the cap. Overrides are tightly controlled through the Clerk, ensuring transparency and accountability. Regular reporting guarantees early detection of overspend trends.
