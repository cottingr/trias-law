---
id: REF-004
title: Precise Referral Cost Tracking and Caps
domain: fiscal
status: proposed
scope: referrals
rules:
  - cost_rate: "Referral cost is charged at $0.30 per minute of active processing."
  - max_cost_per_referral: "No single referral may exceed $500 in cost; longer referrals are automatically split or escalated."
  - monitoring: "Real‑time cost dashboards are visible to Intake; exceeding 80% of the per‑referral cap triggers a warning."
  - escrow: "Referral budgets are pre‑funded; any overrun halts the referral until additional funds are allocated."
---
By codifying referral billing rates and imposing per‑referral caps, this policy curtails unchecked advisory spending while preserving the ability to handle complex cases through escalation mechanisms.
