---
id: INC-005
title: Incident Recurrence Flagging and Cost Control
domain: fiscal
status: proposed
scope: incident-management
rules:
  - recurrence_detection: "If an incident pattern repeats within 30 days, the system flags it as a recurrence and prohibits re‑diagnosis without a root‑cause fix."
  - cost_penalty: "Repeated unaddressed incidents incur a surcharge of $100 per recurrence to incentivize remediation."
  - reporting: "A monthly recurrence report is sent to the Clerk and Intake teams for review."
---
This policy reduces wasteful repeat diagnoses by automatically detecting recurring incidents, applying a cost penalty, and surfacing trends for proactive remediation.
