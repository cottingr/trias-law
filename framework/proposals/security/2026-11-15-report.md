# security — legislative report for 2026-11-15

## What I observed
Audit logs from the period 2026-11-15 show several instances of over‑privileged access: CI pipelines were granted read access to the entire secret store rather than only the secrets they use; the service account named "deployment-bot" possessed write permissions on all cloud storage buckets; and privileged administrative tokens were issued with lifetimes exceeding 24 hours without multi‑factor authentication. These findings indicate violations of the least‑privilege principle and increase the attack surface.

## Open risks
- CI pipelines can read unrelated secrets, risking credential leakage.
- Deployment‑bot can write to any bucket, enabling data exfiltration or tampering.
- Long‑lived admin tokens without MFA increase the chance of token compromise.
