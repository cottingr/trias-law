# fiscal — legislative report for 2026-11-15

## What I observed
On 2026-11-15 the ledger showed total spend at 92% of the monthly cap, with the AI‑frontier tier consuming 48% of that budget. Run submissions clustered in three 15‑minute bursts, each exceeding the average of 45 runs/minute and triggering queue delays. Referral diagnostics ran an average of 8 minutes, 22% above the target 6‑minute window, and incurred unexpected surcharge fees. Two timed‑out runs were recorded with a zero charge, violating the honesty rule that all run time must be billed at least at the minimum rate.

## Open risks
- Budget overshoot risk: spend within 8% of cap could trigger automatic throttling.
- Run‑burst risk: unregulated submission bursts overload the scheduler, increasing latency.
- Referral cost inflation: diagnostics exceed time box and accrue extra fees.
- Zero‑charge timeout risk: billing integrity compromised by unbilled usage.
