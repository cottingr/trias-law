# fiscal — legislative report for 2026-11-22

## What I observed
During the reporting period ending 2026‑11‑22, the ledger shows the frontier model‑execution tier consuming 88 % of its monthly budget, nearing the soft cap imposed in the prior quarter. Run timestamps reveal clustering of high‑volume inference batches between 14:00‑16:00 UTC, pushing the rate‑limit to 95 % of its allowed calls. Referral diagnostics incurred $12,400 in chargeable minutes, with 7 % of runs timing out; two of those timed‑out runs were recorded with a $0.00 charge, violating the cost‑honesty rule. Re‑diagnoses of similar incidents were logged repeatedly, suggesting missed reminders for existing incidents.

## Open risks

- Rate‑limit clustering during peak hours may trigger throttling.
- Zero‑cost timed‑out referral runs undermine cost honesty.
- Repeated re‑diagnoses of incidents increase advisory costs.
