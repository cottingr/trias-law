# fiscal — legislative report for 2026-10-25

## What I observed
During the fiscal period ending 2026-10-25 the system logged a total spend of $9.2M against a monthly cap of $10M (92% utilization). Run submissions spiked to 1,800 runs per hour, a 120% increase over the 30‑day average, causing queue latency spikes of up to 45 seconds. Referral processes averaged 45 minutes each and incurred $0.30 per minute, resulting in $13.5K of referral cost that exceeded the prior‑month average by 38%. Three timed‑out runs were recorded with a $0 charge, violating the honest‑billing rule that all runs must be billed at least the minimum unit cost. Additionally, 27 incidents were re‑diagnosed instead of being flagged as recurring, inflating diagnostic effort and cost.

## Open risks
- Budget cap breach risk – continued spend growth may exceed the $10M ceiling.
- Run clustering risk – high‑volume bursts strain compute resources and increase latency.
- Referral cost overruns – long‑running referrals inflate advisory expenses.
- Zero‑charge timeout leak – billing omissions create revenue leakage and fairness issues.
- Unremediated incident recurrence – repeated diagnoses waste resources and obscure root causes.
