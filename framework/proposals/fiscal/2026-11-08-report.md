# fiscal — legislative report for 2026-11-08

## What I observed
During the reporting period 2026-11-08 the ledger showed that total spend on the frontier model tier approached its daily ceiling (98% of the configured cap) while batch tier usage lingered well below its limit. Run submissions clustered in two‑minute bursts, causing the rate‑limit subsystem to exceed its soft threshold 12 times. Several referral requests ran for longer than the allotted 30‑minute time‑box, yet the system billed them as zero‑cost time‑outs. Re‑diagnosis of recurring incidents was performed instead of simply flagging the repeat, consuming additional advisory resources.

## Open risks
- Frontier model tier spend may breach its daily cap, leading to uncontrolled cost overruns.
- Burst clustering of runs can overload the scheduler and increase latency.
- Referral processes exceeding their time‑boxes are not penalized, encouraging slippage.
- Timed‑out runs are sometimes recorded with zero charge, creating revenue leakage.
- Repeated incidents are re‑diagnosed rather than flagged, wasting advisory cycles.
