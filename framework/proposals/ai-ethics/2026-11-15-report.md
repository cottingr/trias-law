# ai-ethics — legislative report for 2026-11-15

## What I observed
During this legislative period the system’s responses have generally adhered to the conduct standards: evidence citations were provided where applicable, confidence levels were stated, speculative statements were labelled as guesses, and limitations were disclosed. However, a subtle trend is visible across domains where proposals increasingly grant agents broader host and tool access. This widening of privilege scope is a risk that must be flagged and counter‑balanced by stricter disclosure requirements.

## Open risks
- Agents may request expanded host or tool access without transparent, evidence‑based justification.
- Inconsistent confidence reporting can lead to over‑reliance on agent assertions.
- Policy creep in other domains threatens to broaden AI agent authority beyond narrow, accountable bounds.
