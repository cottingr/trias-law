# reliability — legislative report for 2026-11-15

## What I observed
During the reporting period (2026-11-15) the reliability team recorded several recurring safety incidents and monitoring gaps:
1. Two database shard migrations caused brief outages because post‑change health checks on replica lag were missing.
2. An unsanctioned restart of the shared Redis cache service led to a sudden spike in cache‑miss latency and downstream API errors.
3. Simultaneous manual updates of both members of a redundant pair produced a split‑brain condition, briefly corrupting service state.
4. A medium‑plus‑risk infrastructure change was applied without a documented rollback plan, forcing an emergency manual rollback.
These patterns reveal systematic weaknesses in change‑safety approvals, post‑change verification, and monitoring coverage.

## Open risks
- Missing automated post‑change health checks for database migrations.
- Insufficient approval workflow for shared service restarts.
- Lack of coordination when updating redundant pair members.
- Incomplete monitoring of cache‑miss latency and error rates.
